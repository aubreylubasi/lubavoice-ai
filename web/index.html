      How to read the pronunciation

      Every word is respelled with English sounds. Syllables are split with dashes, and the CAPITAL syllable is the one to stress. Greek always marks it with an accent (ά, έ, ί…).

      alike the “a” in father, short
      elike the “e” in bed
      eelike the “ee” in see, short
      olike the “o” in more, short and round
      oolike the “oo” in food, short
      dhthe “th” in this (Greek δ)
      ththe “th” in think (Greek θ)
      gha soft g with air through it, no full stop (Greek γ before a, o, oo)
      khthe “ch” in Scottish loch (Greek χ)
      ha light, forward “h” as in huge (χ before e/ee)
      ythe “y” in yes
      rone quick tap of the tongue, like in Bemba or Spanish
      ks, pssay both sounds, even at the start: KSAN-thee
      ts, dzas in cats, roads
      EE-aa dash between vowels means two separate beats

      Example: ευχαριστώ → ef-kha-ree-STO. The guide is rule-based, so a few words may differ slightly from natural speech. Always copy the voice when they disagree.

      Fast routine for each line

      1. Listen once, then Slow. Tap any hard word to hear it alone.
      2. Say it out loud 2–3 times using the respelling.
      3. Tap Record. Stay quiet during the 1-second countdown, read the whole line, then stay quiet. It stops itself.
      4. Green means Accept & next. Amber or red means read the tip and Retake.

      For your FunCrowd task

      FunCrowd records inside its own app, so your final takes are made there. Use LubaVoice to learn each line and practise until the checks are green, then record it in FunCrowd. You can use split screen on Android: LubaVoice releases the microphone as soon as a take ends.
      • FunCrowd shows a 1-second silence countdown. Wait for it, and pause about a second after the last word.
      • Read exactly what is written, including openers like Συγγνώμη, Λοιπόν, Άκου,.
      • Use a quiet room with no fan, TV or people talking. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
      • Speak at a natural, steady pace, one line in one breath.
      • Each task's own instructions in the FunCrowd app come first if they say something different.
